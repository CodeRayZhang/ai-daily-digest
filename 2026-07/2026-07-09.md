**1. OpenAI 发布 GPT‑Live：实时语音 AI 面向 9 亿用户**

   🔗 https://noma.security/blog/gitlost-how-we-tricked-githubs-ai-agent-into-leaking-private-repos/

   🔗 https://mistral.ai/news/robostral-navigate/

**4. 特朗普政府解除对 OpenAI GPT-5.6 的限制**

   🔗 https://www.reuters.com/world/beijing-is-looking-curbing-overseas-access-chinas-top-ai-models-sources-say-2026-07-07/

**6. arXiv 论文：多智能体 AI 安全——部署规则而非模型本身才是关键**

   🔗 http://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07695v1
